---------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT OVERVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------

The Worship Team Scheduler is a cloud-based scheduling platform that automatically creates, validates, optimizes, and manages church ministry schedules.

The goal is to replace manual spreadsheet scheduling with an intelligent scheduling engine capable of handling availability, ministry rules, assignment fairness, and automatic conflict detection.

The scheduler must support churches of different sizes and should not be hardcoded for only one organization.

The architecture must allow future ministries to be added without modifying the scheduling engine.

---------------------------------------------------------
CORE OBJECTIVES
---------------------------------------------------------

The scheduling engine must ensure that:

• No unavailable member is scheduled.
• Every ministry rule is automatically enforced.
• Workloads are distributed fairly.
• Every service contains the required members.
• Conflicts are automatically detected.
• Replacement suggestions are automatically generated.
• Schedules can be validated before publication.
• Coordinators spend minutes instead of hours preparing schedules.

---------------------------------------------------------
SCHEDULING RULES
---------------------------------------------------------

The scheduling engine validates every assignment.

Mandatory Rules

1.

Unavailable members cannot be assigned.

2.

Inactive members cannot be assigned.

3.

Every member has a configurable maximum monthly assignment count.

Default:

3

4.

A member cannot occupy multiple roles during the same service unless explicitly allowed.

5.

Exactly one Worship Leader.

6.

Required number of backup singers must be satisfied.

7.

Only qualified members may perform their assigned role.

8.

Instrument assignments must satisfy ministry constraints.

Example:

One drummer

One pianist

One bassist

At least one guitarist

Optional second guitarist

9.

Assignment balancing.

The engine should prefer members with fewer assignments.

10.

Leader rotation.

Avoid assigning the same worship leader repeatedly.

11.

Cooldown rule.

Avoid scheduling members on consecutive weeks when alternatives exist.

12.

Conflict explanations.

Every detected conflict must include:

Reason

Severity

Recommendation

13.

Replacement suggestions must satisfy all validation rules.

DEVOTION ROTATION
---------------------------------------------------------

Devotion uses a sequential rotation rather than random assignment.

The system should:

Maintain rotation history.

Resume where the previous month ended.

Prefer members already attending that week's service.

Skip unavailable members.

Prevent immediate repetition.
