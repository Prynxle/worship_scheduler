
Worship Team Scheduler SaaS
Overview
The Worship Team Scheduler is a web-based SaaS platform that automatically creates, validates, and manages church ministry schedules while respecting ministry rules, member availability, fairness, and role assignments.
Instead of manually checking spreadsheets, ministry leaders simply enter member availability, scheduling rules, and service dates. The system automatically detects conflicts, suggests replacements, balances workloads, and generates printable schedules.

Core Objective
Generate valid worship schedules while ensuring:
No unavailable member is scheduled.
Workloads are fairly distributed.
Ministry rules are enforced automatically.
Leaders spend seconds instead of hours creating schedules.
